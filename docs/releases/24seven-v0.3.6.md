# 24seven 0.3.6 for Android

Open the new sidebar from the calendar’s top-right menu.

- Browse months and tap a date to jump to it. Dots show days with visible appointments.
- Show or hide calendars, subscriptions, and categories. Tap **Only** to show one calendar or category.
- Create an appointment on the selected date, open Tasks, or check an account’s sync status.

Calendar and category management open in your browser. Opening the matching settings section requires the updated web app. Subscription error messages require the updated server.
