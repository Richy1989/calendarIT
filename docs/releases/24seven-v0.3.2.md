_Changes since `24seven-v0.3.1`._

✨ New Features

- Widget settings get their own page — Settings → Widgets: add a widget to the home screen and set its look, with a live preview. Changes apply to every placed widget when you tap Save.
- Lines between days in the month widget, in the theme's colour or one you pick, from barely visible to fully transparent.
- Today stands out in the month widget: its whole cell is filled, in a colour and strength you choose.

🔧 Improvements

- The month widget shows new appointments and changed settings right away instead of sometimes showing the old ones for a while.
- Your previous widget background setting is kept.
