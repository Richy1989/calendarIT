_Changes since `v0.9.0`._

✨ New Features

- Quick create — Drag across the grid, double-click a slot or press "+ New", and a small form opens right beside it (a bottom sheet on phones): title, time, repeat, calendar and category, guests, location and description. The appointment appears in the grid as you type. Ctrl+Enter saves, Esc closes, and "More options" opens the full editor with everything you entered.
- Repeat presets — "Every weekday", "Weekly on Tuesday", "Monthly on day 14" and more, named after the appointment's own day; "Custom…" still offers every pattern.
- Default reminders per calendar — Set them once in Settings → Calendars, and every new timed appointment in that calendar starts with them. You can still change them for any single appointment.
- Themes — Choose Midnight, Daylight (a light theme) or Graphite in Settings → General → Appearance, and pick your own accent and highlight colors. Your choice follows you to every device.

🔧 Improvements

- The full editor is tidier: one row per field with an icon, reminders as chips, fewer hint texts.
- The category list shows each category's color before you pick it.
- A one-day appointment's time fits on one line; longer ones read "from … until …".
- The current-time line has a dot at its start.

🔒 Security

- Updated the mail library (MailKit 4.18.2), which fixes a known vulnerability in a cryptography package it uses.
- The Docker Compose setup runs the app with a read-only file system and only the permissions it needs.
- Every release is now tested before it is built, and the published image carries an SBOM and build provenance.

🐛 Fixes

- Outlook invitations and imports could land hours off (for example "(UTC+01:00) Amsterdam, Berlin…" or "Customized Time Zone") — they now arrive at the right time, in the right zone.
- Time zones written in an unusual way (lower case, Windows names like "W. Europe Standard Time", or with a vendor prefix) are recognized, so repeating appointments no longer drift at daylight-saving changes.
- Editing a repeating appointment could drop its deleted and edited occurrences although its time hadn't changed — fixed.
- All-day appointments: a phone west of UTC could miss them, the last day of a multi-day one could be cut off, and their reminders came at midnight UTC and named a clock time — they now fire at midnight where the appointment was made and name the date.
- Invitations you forward to your connected mailbox were ignored — they arrive again, as pending for you to accept or decline.

📦 Deployment & Docs

- Your data comes along: the database upgrades itself on first start (new, empty columns for default reminders and appearance) and keeps everything — checked on SQLite and PostgreSQL with data from 0.9.0.
- No new settings.
