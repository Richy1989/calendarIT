_Changes since `24seven-v0.4.0`._

✨ New Features

- Profile — Each 24seven account has a **Profile** page (Settings → Accounts → the account's card): add, change or remove your picture, and see your email address.
- Change your password in the app. Your other devices and browsers are signed out; this phone stays signed in.
- See where your account is signed in — the app, browsers, other phones, with when each was last used — and sign out any of them, or all except this phone.
- Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen, or in the "Sign in again" dialog, to have a reset link mailed to you. Open it in a browser, set a new password, and sign in with it.

📦 Deployment & Docs

- The profile page and reset links are for 24seven servers; CalDAV accounts manage these on their own server.
- Reset mails need the server to have `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` set and a mail account to send from.
- Needs Android 14 or newer.
