_Changes since `24seven-v0.3.4`._

🔧 Improvements

- A compact appointment editor — Creating or editing an appointment now opens a sheet from the bottom instead of a full screen: title, start and end, all day, repeat, calendar, category and location at a glance, with Save always in reach. "More options" adds notes and reminders; they open by themselves when an appointment already has some.
- The calendar and category pickers show the colour of what's picked.
- Moving an appointment to a calendar of another account clears its category (categories belong to one account).
