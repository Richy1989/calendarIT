_Changes since `v8.0.0` (which was 0.8.0 — see the version note below)._

✨ New Features

- Edit a single occurrence — Change, move or delete just one occurrence of a repeating appointment; the rest of the series stays as it is. Drag an occurrence on the grid to move only that one, and "Reset to series" puts it back. Edited occurrences sync to your phone over CalDAV and travel in .ics files and email invitations.
- Custom repeats — Every N days, weeks, months or years, on the weekdays you pick, monthly on "the second Tuesday", ending on a date or after a number of times. Made for shift patterns like "every 4 weeks on Mon and Tue". (#1)
- Calendar categories — Give a whole calendar a category, say an imported holiday calendar, and everything in it without a category of its own takes that color. Set it on each calendar, when creating one, or right in the import dialog — with "+ New category" on the spot. (#2)
- Signed-in devices — Settings → Security lists every browser signed in to your account. Sign one out, all the others, or everywhere; it takes effect immediately.
- Reliable email — Invitations, replies, reminders and password-reset mails are queued and sent in the background, and retried while your mail server can't be reached. Settings → Email → Outgoing mail shows what was sent and why anything wasn't, with Retry and Discard.

🔧 Improvements

- Saving an appointment never waits for your mail server.
- Problems you can fix are explained (an unreadable repeat rule, an end before the start) instead of failing silently.
- Phone sync keeps edited and deleted occurrences, and a very long title no longer breaks a sync or an import.
- A clean server log: one short, colored line per request, easy to read in `docker logs` and in Unraid's log view. Set `NO_COLOR` for plain text.

🔒 Security

- Sign-in no longer gives away which email addresses have an account, and repeated failed logins from phone sync are slowed down.
- Signing out ends the session on the server too; changing your password signs out every device at once.
- Security headers are sent however you deploy, and mail servers or push endpoints can no longer point into your server's own network (`MAIL_HOST_POLICY`).
- Password-reset links stay out of every log, and profile pictures must be real images.

🐛 Fixes

- One broken repeating appointment could stop reminders for everyone — rules are now checked when saved, and a bad one can't stop anything.
- With PostgreSQL, edits from a phone were refused as conflicts — fixed.
- A phone sending an edited occurrence first could replace its whole series — fixed.
- Deleted days of a repeating all-day event could come back after a daylight-saving change — fixed.
- Two open tabs refreshing at the same moment could sign you out everywhere — fixed.
- The container now stops cleanly instead of being killed after 10 seconds.
- The compose image's health check never reported healthy — fixed.

📦 Deployment & Docs

- Your data comes along: the database upgrades itself on first start and keeps everything (checked on SQLite and PostgreSQL with data from the previous version).
- The app now runs as an unprivileged user inside the container and takes over existing data folders automatically. New Unraid installs run as 99:100; an existing Unraid container runs as 1654 unless you add `PUID=99` / `PGID=100` to it.
- New optional settings: `MAIL_HOST_POLICY`, `PUID` / `PGID`, `NO_COLOR` — see the README.
- Version note: the previous release was tagged `v8.0.0` by mistake; it was 0.8.0, and versions continue from 0.9.0. If you pinned the Docker image to `8.0.0`, switch to `0.9.0` or `latest`.
