_Changes since `24seven-v0.3.0`._

✨ New Features

- See-through month widget — Settings → Month widget has a "Widget background" slider: from solid down to fully transparent, with a soft glass edge so the widget sits on your wallpaper.

🔧 Improvements

- The day view names the whole day at the top, e.g. "Thursday 12".
- Swiping sideways in the calendar always moves to the next or previous month, week or day; the menu opens with its button (and is still swiped shut).
